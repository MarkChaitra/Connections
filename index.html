How to play

Find four groups of four words that share something in common.

  • Select four words, then press Submit.
  • Correct guesses reveal the category.
  • Wrong guesses shake the tiles so you can try again.
  • Click a selected tile again to deselect it.
  • Colours run from easiest (yellow) to hardest (purple).
